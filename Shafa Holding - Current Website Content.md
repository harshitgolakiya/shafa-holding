OUR BUSINESS

Established in 1982 under the leadership of 
founder and current chairman Mr Abdoshamakh
Nasser Alshebani, Shafa has gradually expanded 
from a small-subcontractor with less than 20 
employees to a major international player with over 
a thousand employees. With the skills and 
expertise we have gained over 35 years we offer 
the best and most competitive services to our 
clients.
We are headquartered in Dubai, UAE and operate 
in seven countries across three continents.
Shafa Al Nahdah Building Contracting LLC

Shafa began its operations as a high-quality supplier 
of concrete in 2003. Since then we have embarked 
on various high-capacity projects. We have set up on-
site concrete batching plants in many countries 
around the world, some in remote locations, and 
developed a team of experts and contacts who deliver 
concrete to client needs and specifications. We also 
have in-house laboratories to ensure the concrete is 
produced to the highest standards of quality.
Staying mindful of our environment, we make all 
efforts to ensure that dust and noise emissions are 
controlled while water is recycled and any waste 
concrete is either reused on-site works or disposed of 
safely. Our main commercial plant is located in Dubai 
Maritime City.

With the growing demand for quality Halal poultry in the 
UK. SHAFA HOLDING has acquired one of the oldest 
100% halal facility located in Warwickshire, United 
Kingdom. SHAFA has completely transformed the 
processing plant with new efficient processing lines 
equipped with the latest technology in the industry today. 
The factory’s capacity has more than doubled from the 
previous capacity to cater for the demand of our existing 
customers and new customers with 100% high-quality 
halal HMC-certified products. Making us the UK’s largest 
non-stun, non-gas halal processing facility.
SHAFA has integrated portioning and added value 
packing to have complete integrated processing at one 
facility catered to our client’s needs. This eliminates 
unnecessary transport and with the combination of 
efficient machinery, reduces the company’s carbon 
footprint emissions. Visit our website to find out more.

SHAFA AGRO is our subsidiary established in the 
northern highlands of Iringa, Tanzania. A clear distinction 
from SHAFA’s general manufacturing and construction 
industry. SHAFA Agro is our concrete commitment to the 
earth and contribution to the sustainability of our world 
specifically in Africa.
Our concept is to establish a sustainable and self-
sufficient, world-class food supply chain in Africa derived 
from soil and soil quality. With the infrastructure for water 
security and feed security, we are here to make and lead 
sustainable change in the farming industry. We strive to 
become the foundation and a benchmark of African 
Farming in the animal feed industry, poultry industry, 
dairy industry and livestock. Creating a sustainable and 
efficient supply chain for Tanzanian farmers and being a 
pivotal part of our region’s food security and social 
economic growth. Visit our website to learn more.
